Alongside analytics, I am **President and Club Coach of the University of Nottingham Powerlifting Club**. Previously, I worked as a **GPS Data Analyst Intern with the University of Nottingham Men’s Hockey 1st Team**, analysing athlete GPS data during training and competition.

The role involved monitoring physical performance metrics, providing real-time feedback and translating performance data into practical information that could support coaching and match-day decisions.
